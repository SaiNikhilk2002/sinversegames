## Behaviour preserved

- **Golden replays.** 112 campaign replays were generated by the original engine, then rerun after every change: 7 kitchens, 4 seeds, and clean, human, messy and off-heat play. Score, stars, every accepted and rejected action, and final state are unchanged.
- **Mutation checks.** The fixture first missed one of three deliberate rule changes, the off-heat cooking rate. I added the off-heat play style, regenerated the fixture from the original engine, and then all three changes failed the tests.
- **Old saved runs.** Runs saved before this work earn seals, and render without the new details.
- **Files outside this work.** The original Cuisine Quest test files and the root `AGENTS.md` and `CLAUDE.md` weren't changed. The Cricket 2 branch and worktree weren't touched, and the two branches changed no files in common.

### New browser coverage

- Chef Rivals:
  - Two browsers get the same theme and ingredient.
  - Each sees the other's chef move.
  - Both cook at once, reach the judging, and see identical totals and one consistent headline.
  - Rematch works, and so does the 320 px layout.
  - On a 360×640 phone the controls are in view, and the exact kitchen comes back after a reload.
- Kitchen Rush Co-op:
  - One player grills while the other plates, each seeing the other's cook.
  - When both reach for the same plate at once, exactly one gets it.
  - The order is served once, and both browsers see the same team result.
  - On a phone, every control is reachable.
- Platform:
  - The menu shows only playable modes and each one opens.
  - Back and forward navigation keep the right URL, and route fades leave no transform behind.
  - Low effects and reduced motion persist across reload.
  - The OS reduced-motion preference removes the route fade.
- Campaign: a real shift earns rank, seals and mastery. That is confirmed on the map, after a reload and on the profile, at 320 px, and when goals fail to load.

### New engine and socket coverage, highlights

- **Rivals:**
  - Both chefs get identical kitchens, and one chef's actions never touch the other's.
  - The server clock is the only time source.
  - Forged finishes, extra score fields, out-of-date tabs, stale matches and retried actions are rejected or deduplicated.
  - Good cooking beats fast clicking, and speed is capped at 5% of the score.
  - Different ticket orders are equally valid.
  - Ties, departures, aborts, reconnects and matchmaking all behave as specified.
- **Co-op:**
  - Every kitchen can be finished by teams of 2 and 4.
  - First tap wins a station or cooked food. A plate can't be held twice, and an order can't be completed twice.
  - Stock can't be over-drawn, and flare-ups block and then clear.
  - Combos only pay in combo kitchens, and teamwork points are capped.
  - Future guests are hidden from the view, and a departing cook's plate is freed.
  - Solo Kitchen Rush is unchanged.

## Performance measured

| Replay-goal query at its 500-run limit, local PostgreSQL, 15 samples | Median 5.2 ms, max 13.3 ms |

Remote Neon latency will be higher and wasn't measured.

Also not measured: Worker bundle size, physical devices, frame rates for the new screens, and network traffic per match. Duel and co-op screens repaint at most ten times a second and animate chefs with CSS transforms only.

## Known limitations

- Linux `pnpm cloudflare:build` wasn't run on this branch, because WSL builds have been unreliable on this laptop. The new games add no dependencies and no server-only code to the web bundle, but the Worker build is unverified.
- No physical-device, Safari or screen-reader testing.
- Rivals is 1v1 only. Co-op kitchens aren't saved as progress.
- Chef looks are generated from the player ID. There's no customization screen yet.
- The new games need web and realtime deployed together, and that hasn't been verified on Railway.
- Bots can play well, and server time and rate limits only limit scripted play, not prevent it. Rewards stay capped by the existing rules.
